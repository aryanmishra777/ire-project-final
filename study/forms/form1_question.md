# Form 1: Your literature-search question

*Set this up as a Google Form. Takes about 3 minutes.*

---

**About this study.** This is a course project for CS4.406 (Information Retrieval and
Extraction, IIIT Hyderabad). It tests whether letting an AI rephrase a research question
before searching helps you find relevant papers. You'll write one question now. In a day or
two you'll get a second form with about 20 papers to rate, which takes about 15 minutes.

**What happens to your answers.**
- Your question is stored under an anonymous ID (P01, P02, …), never with your name.
- It's processed on a local model on my laptop and isn't sent to any hosted AI service.
- Your answers are used only for this course project and deleted after grading.
- You can withdraw at any time by emailing me; your answers will then be deleted.

- [ ] I've read the above and agree to take part. *(required)*

---

relevant paper when you saw one.

large ones, without a lot of labelled data?"*
